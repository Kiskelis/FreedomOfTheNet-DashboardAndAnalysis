# 🌐 Freedom on the Net Analysis (2020–2024)

This dashboard explores a topic I’ve been personally curious about - how internet freedom is changing around the world.  
Over five years of Freedom House data were transformed into a dynamic Power BI report that helps track trends, highlight risks, and point to emerging opportunities for digital rights, infrastructure, and secure access tools.

It’s designed with three main audiences in mind:
- **VPN providers** and privacy-first tech companies
- **Policy groups, NGOs, and journalists**
- **Hiring managers reviewing my portfolio**

---

## 🔍 Key Insights & Trends (2020–2024)

- ### 📈 **Global Freedom Is Gradually Improving**  
  On average, freedom scores went up by **+0.3 points per year**, rising from **51.69 to 52.86** - a modest but consistent gain.

- ### 🟠 **The Freedom Divide Is Growing**  
  Countries like **Iceland, Estonia, and Canada** continue to lead, while others - especially in **Asia and Central Eurasia** - are falling behind or tightening controls.

- ### 🌍 **Some Years See Progress, Others Regression**  
  In **2022**, 26 countries improved (the most in this period), but the global average still dropped. In contrast, **2024** saw fewer countries improve (18), but the overall score bounced back.

- ### 🚨 **Europe Is Slipping Back**  
  Europe is the only continent that saw a **net score decline** over the five years. This reflects subtle but serious backsliding in democratic protections.

- ### 🟩 **South America Saw the Strongest Gains**  
  With an **11.11% score improvement**, the region stands out. Countries like **Ecuador** and **Brazil** are becoming increasingly open - and represent real growth potential for privacy-based tools.

---

## 🧠 Strategic Recommendations

### 🔐 For VPN Providers:

- Focus attention on:
  - **Ecuador, Brazil, and Thailand** – improving but still have risks and restrictions.
  - **Gambia and South Africa** – mobile-first populations with increasing awareness of digital privacy.

- **🌐 Urban Asia as a Growth Opportunity**  
  While much of **Asia remains Not Free**, countries like **Malaysia**, **Indonesia**, and parts of **India** are showing demand for private access - especially on mobile.  
  → Focus on **affordable, lightweight VPN solutions** optimized for Android.

- **📉 Target Declining Democracies Before They Tighten Further**  
  Countries such as Hungary, and Turkey are experiencing a slow decline in digital freedoms.  
  → Position early with **“freedom-first” messaging** before further restrictions limit distribution.

- **📢 Launch Education Campaigns Where Censorship Is Rising**  
  In countries like **Myanmar** or **Vietnam**, censorship is increasing but awareness of secure tools is still low.  
  → Partner with local NGOs, educators, or media to build trust and teach the basics of digital safety.
